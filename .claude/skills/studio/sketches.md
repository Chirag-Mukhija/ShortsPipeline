# The creator's sketches

The creator draws rough sketches on the iPad of how they see a scene and adds them to the video's inbox. Each one is listed in `inbox/index.json` with its intent and note; a `for a beat` sketch's note reads `Beat N: Want: <what the creator wants on that beat> From the sketch: <what to take from the drawing>`. **Want** is the instruction, and the sketch is context for it. Take from the sketch exactly what **From the sketch** says, for example only the layout, and nothing more. If that part is empty, use the whole sketch. Open every image with Read and look at it; don't go by the filename.

On a music-only video (`Audio: music` in `status.md`), sketches matter even more, because the visuals are the whole video. A sketch of a style frame (the opening frame, the peak or the last frame) sets the look for every beat around it.

## When they arrive
- **Before the plan:** a sketch of the overall flow. Build the Structure from it.
- **At the plan gate (the usual time):** the creator sketches against the beat sheet in `plan.md`. Approving the plan doesn't wait for sketches.
- **With notes that redo the plan:** if the redo adds, removes or reorders beats, match each `for a beat` sketch to its new beat by what the sketch is about, not by its old number. List the moves under the beat sheet as `Sketches moved: …`. If a sketch's beat was cut, say so and ask where it belongs.
- **During the script gate:** the scriptwriter folds it into that beat's `[ON SCREEN]` on the next revision. The plan isn't redone, unless the sketch changes what a beat *says* or the order of beats. In that case, ask first.

## How to treat a sketch
1. **The creator's idea wins.** Keep its composition, its metaphor and what it shows. You may tighten it so it reads better on screen, but don't swap it for your own idea.
2. **Fill in what's undefined, silently.** Rough sketches leave a lot open: colors, type, exact layout, motion, timing, labels. Take these from the plan's Look and feel, and replace vague or off-palette colors with the plan's palette. No need to ask about any of this.
3. **Push back only when a sketch is clearly bad.** That means at least one of these is true:
   - It contradicts or muddles what the beat is saying.
   - It can't be read at phone size: too much text or too many elements for the time the beat is on screen.
   - It shows something false, or a claim that isn't in `research/sources.md`.
   - It needs footage, logos or images the creator doesn't own or can't license.
   - It can't be built with what the studio has.

   Never push back on taste. A plain or unusual choice is the creator's call. When a sketch does fail, keep it in the script as drawn, then propose one concrete alternative and say which rule it breaks. The creator chooses at the gate (in gated mode, put it in `questions.json` with "Keep my sketch" as the first option).
4. **Credit the sketch:** `[ON SCREEN: GRAPHIC: … (from your sketch inbox/<file>)]`.
5. **Log it** in a `## Sketch notes` section at the end of `script.md`, one line per sketch:
   - what you used it for
   - what you filled in
   - any pushback

   The creator reads this at the gate.
