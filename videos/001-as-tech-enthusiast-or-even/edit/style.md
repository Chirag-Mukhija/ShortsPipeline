# Style brief: video 001

- **Theme skin:** hand-drawn (the creator's choice at the plan gate). Off-white dot-grid iPad paper, graphite ink, Caveat handwriting, Inter for numbers and source labels. Tokens live in `edit/scenes/style.ts`.
- **Accent:** one coral, kept for the one thing that matters most in a shot (the key word, the "asked why" bar, the DSA thread, the main link). Everything else is graphite or pencil grey.
- **Surface recipe:** paper base (warm off-white plus dot grid), paper grain, soft vignette outside the camera rig.
- **Life language:** pencil strokes boil (they re-jitter every 4 frames), so even holds are alive. Plus base camera drift.
- **Emphasis form:** a coral hand-drawn underline that draws 0.4–0.6 s after the words land. Used for nothing else.
- **Caveat voice:** a small pencil margin note for every "that's my judgment / my extension / my version" line. It's the honest, not guru promise in visual form, so it recurs on purpose.
- **Transitions:** overlap inside beats (the default), whips for attention jumps, a mask grown from the robot's eye (3→4), a match cut where the stamped folder becomes a chain link (4→5), one paper turn (5→6), one push-through into the docs book (6→7, the only depth dive), and a pull-back into the main link (7→8). No plain fades.
- **Camera temperament:** energetic. Shots run 2–6 s, with a camera change on about every sentence. Fast moves (whips) stay within a third of the shots, except in Beat 1, where the creator asked for fast moves from frame 0 (2026-11-17).
- **The one special moment:** Beat 5. The whole chain greys out link by link, then one thick coral thread draws under all of it and slams into the DSA tag.
